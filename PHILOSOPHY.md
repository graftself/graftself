# The Philosophy of GraftSelf

## As old as time

People have always wanted a second self that knows them and steers them.

- **Apprenticeship** was the oldest way knowledge moved from one person to another. The apprentice watches, tries, gets corrected, and slowly becomes the master. Every correction you give an AI is one of those moments. Preference training is apprenticeship turned into math.
- **Socrates** spoke of his daimonion, an inner voice that warned him when he was about to go wrong.
- **Journals, letters and family stories** were how people tried to make themselves outlast a single life.
- **The extended mind.** In 1998 the philosophers Andy Clark and David Chalmers argued that a notebook you truly rely on is part of your mind. A graftself is that notebook, and it can think and act.

The urge is ancient. What is new is that one person, at home, can finally build it.

## The grove

Coast redwood roots run shallow, often only six to twelve feet deep, but they spread wide and graft into their neighbors' roots. Where they fuse, water and sugar move from where there is plenty to where there is need. The grove stands through storms as one living thing.

A graftself grafts onto a life the same way. Your emails, messages, code, photos and corrections are the roots. The model is where they fuse into one self.

## Holobiont

In biology a holobiont is a host together with every organism that lives with it, counted as one unit. A person is not only a person. They are their work, their people, their tools and their history, functioning together. GraftSelf models that whole.

## Borrow the biggest brain, then make it yours

Nobody needs to train a giant model from nothing. Research labs spend millions training large open models on trillions of words. GraftSelf borrows the strongest open model that fits on hardware you own, at full precision, and grafts your life onto it.

- **Distillation.** Frontier models act as teachers. They read your history and write lessons: the situation, what you would do, and why. A smaller student learns frontier-level judgment applied to one life.
- **Fine-tuning.** Your own writing goes into the weights, so your voice and habits live there.
- **Preference training.** Every "not this, that" correction teaches judgment.
- **Memory.** Facts that change stay in a private index the model looks up, so it never goes stale.

As hardware grows, the model grows. As you grow, it grows with you, one ring every night.

## Local for the everyday, cloud for the hard stuff

The part that knows you runs on your own machine, private and always available. When a job needs more power than one machine has, it hands the hard part to a bigger model with your context attached and judges the result your way.

## Private by design

Your graftself never leaves your machines. This project shares the recipe and the tools so anyone can grow their own. No one's personal weights or data are ever published.

## Senses and hands

A graftself can see your world through your photos, hear you through your voice, answer in your voice, and work your computers through the same tools you use. Put it in a robot and the robot knows its owner.

## Not trained on everyone. Grafted from you.
